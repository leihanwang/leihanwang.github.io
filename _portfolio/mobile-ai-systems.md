I study **mobile AI system architecture** and efficient inference on smartphone accelerators. This work focuses on understanding how modern NPUs execute neural workloads and how software/runtime design can better exploit hardware capabilities under tight memory, latency, and energy constraints.

Current directions include projects around **NPUGen** and **ShadowNPU**, as well as low-level study of mobile accelerator architectures such as **Qualcomm Hexagon**.

**Research themes:** on-device AI, NPU architecture, efficient inference, mobile systems, LLM systems.
