I have worked on federated learning methods for reliable learning across decentralized and heterogeneous clients.

Research directions include **FedSTAR**, which studies trajectory prediction under spatiotemporal heterogeneity, and **FedHAN**, which investigates robustness under model heterogeneity and label noise. The broader goal is to improve global model stability while preserving useful local information across diverse clients.

**Research themes:** federated learning, heterogeneity, robustness, contrastive alignment, trustworthy AI.
